# Test plan

| Scenario | Steps | Expected | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| `slr_polygon_scanline` | Fill 45 fixed-seed and hand-built polygons on 167×139, one across a band and the canvas edges on 90×200, and degenerate ones | Each mask equals the per-pixel even-odd test; bounds equal the scanned bounds; degenerate ones are empty with no bounds | `pictura-select::scanline_fill_matches_the_per_pixel_test`, `pictura-select::spans_past_a_band_and_the_canvas_edge`, `pictura-select::too_few_points_or_no_area_is_empty` |
| `slr_bounded_ops` | Feather three polygons (one on the edges) at radii 0.5–30 and combine each into a base in every mode | Equal to `feather` and `combine`; coverage stays in the window | `pictura-select::bounded_feather_equals_the_whole_mask_feather`, `pictura-select::bounded_combine_equals_the_whole_mask_combine` |
| `slr_lasso_seeds_bounds` | Commit New, Add, Subtract, Intersect lassos; a feathered lasso; an off-canvas lasso | Selections equal the whole-mask combines; bounds computes stay 0 for New/Add, 1 after Subtract and after the feather, 0 off canvas | `pictura-app::a_lasso_seeds_its_bounds_and_matches_the_whole_mask_combine`, `pictura-app::a_feathered_lasso_equals_the_whole_mask_feather_and_is_rescanned`, `pictura-app::a_lasso_outside_the_canvas_selects_nothing` |
| `slr_magnetic_lazy` | Read every cost of RGB and gray noise, an edge and a flat image at contrasts 1, 10, 60 | Bit-identical to the eager field | `pictura-select::lazy_tiles_equal_the_eager_field` |
| `slr_magnetic_press` | Build a 600×500 field, then trace a short segment | No tile after the build; some but not all after the trace | `pictura-select::a_press_computes_no_tile` |
| `slr_shape_box` | Composite a shape layer (with and without a drop shadow) over a multiply layer with the content box and with the whole rect | Byte-identical; the fill is red | `pictura-render::a_shape_composites_as_its_whole_rect_did` |
| `slr_shape_mask_samples` | Composite a 100×80 ellipse on 300×200 and count mask samples | At most the box's area | `pictura-render::a_shape_samples_its_mask_only_in_its_box` |
| `slr_shape_move` | 800×600: add a 40×40 shape, begin the Move preview, commit a (30, 20) move | Image at (40, 40) at most 42×42; the damaged region at most 80×80; red at (100, 80), white at (45, 45) | `tst_damage_bounds::shapeMoveIsBoundedToTheShape` |
| `slr_shape_path_drag` | 800×600: add a 40×40 shape, target its outline, move the subpath by (30, 20), commit | The damaged region at most 80×80; red at (100, 80), white at (45, 45) | `tst_damage_bounds::shapePathDragRefreshesItsReach` |
| `slr_coverage_box` | Rasterize four ellipses and rectangles into their box and the document; one off the canvas | Expanded boxes equal the document masks; the off-canvas shape has no box | `pictura-core::the_coverage_box_holds_every_covered_pixel` |
| `slr_boxed_fill` | Fill a soft 49×70 box across bands 1–2 of a 90×300 layer at (0,0), at (-5,10), and as a Background, each after a history fracture | Planes and dirty rect equal the document-mask fill; band 1 forked, band 4 shared | `pictura-paint::a_boxed_fill_matches_the_document_mask_and_forks_only_its_bands` |
| `slr_large_manual` | Real display, new 17000×17000 document, `PICTURA_PAINT_TIMING=1`: each lasso, each shape mode, a shape Move, a Path Selection drag | No whole-document stall; correct selection, shapes and positions | manual: the full-size document does not fit the test runners' memory and a stall is felt on a real display; `scripts/profile-tools.py --size 17000x17000` and `pictura-app::shape_lasso_profile` time the stages |
