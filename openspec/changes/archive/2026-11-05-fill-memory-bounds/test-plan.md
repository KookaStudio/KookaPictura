# Test plan

| Scenario | Steps | Expected | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fmb_flood_box` | Flood a 37×29 noise layer at (0,0), at (-5,3), and as a Background over the canvas and a 24×21 box, contiguous and not; flood a planar composite; flood random gates from one seed | Matches equal the surface test; the box flood is the canvas flood's crop; contiguous equals the seed-plane flood of the box | `pictura-paint::a_boxed_flood_reads_the_planes_as_the_surface_does`, `pictura-paint::a_composite_source_reads_its_planes`, `pictura-core::a_single_seed_floods_as_its_seed_plane_does` |
| `fmb_wall` | Select two 6×6 rooms on a white 30×10 layer and flood one, contiguous and not, and from the gap; in the app, select two rooms of a white 40×20 document and click one, then the other with Contiguous off | 36 pixels contiguous, 84 (both rooms) not, nothing from the gap; in the app the clicked room fills, the other only with Contiguous off, the gap stays white | `pictura-paint::the_selection_is_a_wall_for_the_contiguous_flood`, `tst_fill_tools::paintBucketStaysInsideItsSelection` |
| `fmb_antialias_wall` | Flood a uniform 20×20 layer in a diagonal selection and anti-alias it | Every selected box pixel is 255 | `pictura-paint::the_selection_edge_is_not_softened` |
| `fmb_fill_box` | Fill a 49×70 box of a 90×300 layer with no mask and with a full mask | Identical planes and rects; the dirty rect is the box | `pictura-paint::a_fill_without_a_mask_covers_its_box`, `pictura-paint::a_boxed_fill_matches_the_document_mask_and_forks_only_its_bands` |
| `fmb_stroke_window` | Stroke three selections on 48×40 at three alignments and widths 1, 3, 6 | Planes and dirty rect equal the whole-document band | `pictura-paint::a_windowed_stroke_equals_the_whole_document_stroke` |
| `fmb_gradient_box` | Draw a gradient through a box selection on a fractured 40×300 layer with and without `within` | Identical planes and rect; band 4 stays shared | `pictura-paint::a_boxed_gradient_equals_the_unboxed_one_and_forks_only_its_bands` |
| `fmb_gate` | With 64 bytes "available", try a bucket click, a gradient, a Fill and a Stroke; restore and Fill | Each refused with the memory reason (the status bar shows it for the tool), pixels and history unchanged; the restored Fill commits one state | `tst_fill_tools::fillsRefuseWhatMemoryCannotHold`, `pictura-app::the_gate_keeps_a_quarter_of_the_memory_free`, `pictura-app::a_refusal_is_remembered_until_the_next_fill`, `pictura-app::a_write_forks_the_bands_it_crosses` |
| `fmb_large_manual` | On a ~7 GB host, open a 17000×17000 document, draw a rectangular marquee, Paint Bucket inside it; then fill with little memory free | The fill completes without an OOM kill; an over-budget fill shows "Could not fill: not enough memory…" | manual: a 17000×17000 document does not fit the test runners' memory, and the OOM killer is a property of the real host |
