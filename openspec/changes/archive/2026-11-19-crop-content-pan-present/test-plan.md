# Test plan — crop-content-pan-present

| Scenario | Steps | Expected | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| `crop_pan_level_present` | Resize and show a standalone `ImageView`, attach a fake level provider that records every `(level, x, y, w, h)` request and every reported view rectangle, `setDocument(4000×3000)`, `setCropContentOffset(100, 100)`, render at zoom 1.0 and 0.4; repeat once with `setPresentLevelCropForTest(false)` for the fallback draw; render without the offset and compare the reported rectangles; finally render a 520×120 document at 100 % with the offset | Every request fits the viewport (in document pixels) plus the sampling margin (one level pixel per edge plus rounding), no request covers the whole level 0, the reported rectangle moves back by the offset (100 doc px at level 0), and the small document still reports its whole rectangle | `tst_present_cache::cropPanPresentsThroughALevel` |
| `crop_pan_matches_idle` | New 4000×3000 document, paint a non-uniform red dab, render at zoom 1.0 and 0.4; render with `setCropContentOffset(10, 10)`, capture the rebuild count, move the offset to `(20, 20)` and render (pixels differ, count unchanged); then reset the offset, `panBy(+10·zoom, +10·zoom)` and render; at 100 % also render once with `setPresentLevelCropForTest(false)` | The content-offset frame equals the equivalent view-pan frame at both zooms, at 100 % the pan frame also equals the direct level-0 draw, and the present cache is reused across the pan | `tst_present_cache::cropPanMatchesAViewPan` |
| `crop_pan_single_channel` | New 520×120 document, 100 px red dab at the document centre, zoom 1.0; assert the widget centre is pure red and a point just past the dab radius is white; then `setChannelMask(0x2)` (green only), `setCropContentOffset(10, 10)`, render, and sample that same point | The point turns black (the green channel of the red dab, now shifted under it) where it was white before, so the channel keeps being presented and moves with the pan | `tst_present_cache::cropPanKeepsTheSingleChannel` |
