# Test plan — colour-range-extras

Every scenario in the delta spec has one row. Coverage is the last column and
names the automated test that proves it; the new tests are written before the
code they exercise and compiled after the dialog work lands.

## tools/color-range

| Scenario | Steps | Expected | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bands and fuzziness behave | Match red against red and blue, raise Fuzziness on a near miss and a grey ramp, pick Reds and Greens against red, green, and grey, and pick Highlights and Shadows against white and black | The exact colour is selected, Fuzziness widens and softens, grey belongs to no colour band, and the tonal bands split light from dark | `pictura-select::sampled_takes_the_match_and_leaves_the_rest`; `pictura-select::fuzziness_widens_and_softens`; `pictura-select::a_colour_band_goes_by_hue_and_ignores_greys`; `pictura-select::the_tonal_bands_split_light_from_dark` |
| `crx_samples` | Evaluate Sampled Colors with red and blue samples over red, green, and blue pixels, then add a green sample | Red and blue selected and green not before the third sample; all three selected after it; an unmatched colour stays unselected | `pictura-select::every_sample_contributes_its_best_match` |
| `crx_localized` | Sample one end of a one-row red strip at the other end, enable Localized Color Clusters, and evaluate at Range 0, Range 100, with localization off, and with a positionless sample; repeat on a document taller than 256 rows | The far end is unselected at Range 0, selected at Range 100, and selected with localization off; a positionless sample carries no proximity weight; the tall document keeps the sample at its document row rather than its band row | `pictura-select::localized_clusters_fall_off_with_range`; `pictura-select::localized_ignores_a_positionless_sample`; `pictura-app::localized_samples_stay_in_document_space` |
| `crx_skin` | Evaluate Skin Tones on a skin patch, saturated blue, and neutral grey, then raise Fuzziness | Skin selected, blue and grey not, and the skin patch stays selected as Fuzziness rises | `pictura-select::skin_tones_ignore_non_skin_colours` |
| `crx_gamut` | Run the Working CMYK gamut check on a saturated colour outside the profile and a neutral grey inside it, then Invert the result; repeat with a non-sRGB working profile | The saturated pixel is selected and the grey is not; Invert reverses both; the source profile is the document's working profile | `pictura-color::gamut_check_marks_unprintable_colours`; `pictura-app::color_range_gamut_follows_the_proof_setup`; `pictura-app::color_range_gamut_uses_the_working_profile` |
| `crx_alpha` | Evaluate Sampled Colors on a fully transparent pixel with Invert off and on | Unselected with Invert off and selected with Invert on, like an opaque pixel with no matching colour | `pictura-select::transparent_pixels_take_no_coverage` |
| Sample, preview, select, refine | Open the dialog on a red square over white, click the square, switch to Highlights and back, press OK, cancel a second dialog, and run again on a live rectangle | The click samples red without history, the preview is white on the square and black elsewhere and reversed for Highlights, OK records one state, Cancel records nothing, and the refine keeps only red inside the rectangle | `tst_color_range::sampleAndSelect`; `tst_color_range::refinesALiveSelection` |
| `crx_samplers` | Open on separate red and blue squares, click red with Replace, click blue with Add, then Alt-click blue | The preview selects both after Add and only red after the subtract; OK commits the remaining sample | `tst_color_range::samplesAddAndSubtract` |
| `crx_dialog_localized` | Show two separated red squares, sample one, confirm Range is disabled with localization off, then enable Localized Color Clusters and lower then raise Range | Range is disabled while localization is off; the far square is unselected at the low Range and selected at the high Range; Range stays within 0–100 | `tst_color_range::localizedClustersNarrowThePreview` |
| `crx_dialog_skin` | Select Skin Tones on a document with a skin patch and a saturated blue patch | The swatch and eyedroppers disable, the preview selects the skin patch, and blue is excluded | `tst_color_range::skinTonesSelectSkin` |
| `crx_dialog_gamut` | Open the dialog on RGB, select Out Of Gamut, then close and repeat on Grayscale and CMYK documents | Out Of Gamut is offered and selects the unprintable patch only on RGB, and the item is absent on Grayscale and CMYK | `tst_color_range::outOfGamutFollowsTheProofSetup`; `pictura-app::color_range_gamut_follows_the_proof_setup` |
| `crx_dialog_preview` | Switch the dialog's display preview between Selection and Image with the control and with Control/Command | Selection shows the grey coverage mask, Image shows the bounded composite, and the shortcut toggles the same pair with the same settings | `tst_color_range::selectionAndImagePreviews`; `tst_color_range::ctrlTogglesTheDisplayPreview` |
| `crx_canvas_preview` | Cycle the image-window Selection Preview through None, Grayscale, Black Matte, White Matte, and Quick Mask with proof colours on and off, then close the dialog | None leaves the crop unchanged, Grayscale is white on the square, the mattes keep the square over black or white, Quick Mask adds the rubylith over unselected pixels, proof colours do not change the matched coverage, and closing restores the plain crop | `tst_color_range::imageWindowPreviewModes`; `tst_color_range::previewIgnoresProofColors` |
| `crx_save_load` | Save a Sampled Colors configuration with two samples, localized on, Range 30, and Invert on through the path helper; change the controls; load the file | Select mode, Fuzziness, localization, Range, Invert, and the sample colours and positions are restored, and OK reproduces the same selection | `tst_color_range::saveLoadRoundTrip` |
| `crx_save_load_invalid` | Load malformed JSON and a wrong-version settings file | Both loads are refused, the controls and preview are unchanged, and an inline message is shown | `tst_color_range::saveLoadRejectsInvalid` |
| `crx_refine_preview` | With a live rectangle selection, open the dialog, sample red, and read the preview inside and outside the selection | The preview is white only on the matching pixels inside the live selection and matches the intersection OK commits | `tst_color_range::previewReflectsTheLiveSelection` |
