# Test plan

| Scenario | Steps | Expected result | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| thhist_dialog | Open Threshold on a multitone active layer with a small marquee. Change the level and let the preview land. | The histogram sits between the value row and slider, bins only contain the selected opening tone, and preview leaves the bins stable. | `tst_image_adjustments::thresholdHistogramTracksOpeningSelection` |
| thhist_mask | Open Threshold on a raster mask, then on Quick Mask in each polarity, with distinct target tones; change the level and let each preview land. Separately calculate bins with unequal selection coverage over mask tones. | Each dialog displays its actual editable grayscale target, with Quick Mask using working_value in its current polarity, and retains its opening bins during preview; gray bins carry the corresponding selection weights. | `tst_image_adjustments::thresholdHistogramUsesRasterMask`, `tst_image_adjustments::thresholdHistogramUsesQuickMask`, `pictura-app::threshold_histogram_weights_editable_masks` |
| thhist_props | Put Threshold between two layers of different tones and open Properties. | The chart between the value row and slider shows the tone contributed by the lower layer and excludes the upper layer and Threshold output. | `tst_properties_panel::thresholdHistogramShowsLowerLayers` |
| thhist_refresh | Change lower source content and then selection coverage while Properties remains on Threshold, outside a slider gesture. | The displayed bins refresh to reflect each new source and selection. | `tst_properties_panel::thresholdHistogramRefreshesSourceAndSelection` |
| thhist_selection_move | Draw a marquee over one lower-source tone, drag it to another, then cancel the drag. | Properties refreshes on the marquee commit, follows the translated coverage during the drag, and restores the original bins on cancellation. | `tst_properties_panel::thresholdHistogramTracksSelectionMove` |
| thhist_groups | Place Threshold in a pass-through group over a distinctive lower outside source, then in an isolated group with distinctive internal and external lower sources. | The pass-through chart includes contributing lower context; the isolated chart uses its internal lower source and excludes external context. | `tst_properties_panel::thresholdHistogramRespectsGroupBoundaries` |
| thhist_large_selection | Compute bins repeatedly for a small off-origin zero-exterior selection on a large source, with distinct selected and unselected tones. | Results are deterministic, include the selected tones and exclude unselected tones, while tile sampling remains bounded and uses cached mask bounds. | `pictura-app::threshold_histogram_samples_small_selection_on_large_source` |
| thhist_empty | Open Threshold on transparent pixels. | No bars are shown and the slider is available. | `tst_image_adjustments::thresholdHistogramShowsEmptyInput` |
