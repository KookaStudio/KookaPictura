# Test Plan

Covers every scenario in the `ui/layers-panel` delta for
`clamp-layer-thumbnail-aspect`. The clamp is observable in the emitted
thumbnail images and in the delegate card, so both layers are automated; no
manual rows are expected.

| Scenario | Steps | Expected result | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lpr_thumb_aspect` | Show a regular layer of a document between 1:3 and 3:1 in the Layers panel, e.g. 10x20 and 20x10, whose rounded extent already meets the bound | The drawn card and thumbnail image match the document aspect, with the unused slot area empty | `tst_layers_panel_fixes::thumbnailFillsRow`, `tst_layers_panel_fixes::maskThumbnailKeepsDocumentAspectOnSquareRows`, `tst_layers_snapshot::subCanvasLayerPlacement` |
| `lpr_thumb_aspect_clamp` | Show a full-canvas layer on a 200x20 and a 20x200 document at thumbnail size 24; snapshot with Entire Document contents and paint the row; add a raster and a vector mask; show a 160x16 document at size 16; show a 300x100 document at size 16; show a shape layer | Wide: image and card are 24x8 (3:1) with opaque content at the right edge. Tall: the image is 8x24 (1:3), the card is 11x32 (the clamped aspect letterboxed into the 24x32 row slot and rounded up), opaque content at the bottom edge, and both card bounds hold (width and height each at most three times the other). At size 16 the 160x16 image is 16x6 (short edge ceil(16/3)); the 300x100 image and card are 16x6 (the rounded short edge is raised). A shape thumbnail is 24x8 / 8x24. Both masks use the same clamped card and the content fills it, with no clipping or transparent strip | `pictura-app::extreme_document_thumbnail_aspect_is_clamped`, `pictura-app::extreme_document_shape_thumbnail_aspect_is_clamped`, `tst_canvas_mask_domain::extremeAspectThumbnailIsClamped` |
| `lpr_thumb_source_aspect` | Show a full-width layer on a 10x20 (1:2) document with Entire Document contents | The thumbnail image is 12x24 and its rightmost column is layer content, not transparent padding | `pictura-app::positioned_thumbnail_follows_document_aspect`, `tst_layers_panel_fixes::tallThumbnailKeepsDocumentAspect` |
| `lpr_thumb_square_card` | Show an adjustment, fill, type, or content-less synthetic row on a non-square document, including an adjustment and a solid fill row on the 200x20 clamp fixture | Its card is 1:1 while a pixel layer's thumbnail is letterboxed to the clamped document aspect | `tst_layers_panel_fixes::adjustmentThumbnailIsSquareWithoutOutline`, `tst_layers_panel_fixes::fillThumbnailIsSquareWithOutline`, `tst_layers_panel_fixes::typeThumbnailIsSquareWithoutOutline`, `tst_layers_snapshot::fillThumbnailIsSquare`, `tst_canvas_mask_domain::extremeAspectThumbnailIsClamped` |
| `lpr_thumb_sub_canvas_layer` | Create a 20x10 document, paste a 4x4 raster layer at (6,2), snapshot with `entireDocument=true`; separately create a 400x100 document, paste a 50x50 layer at (50,25), and paint the panel | The snapshot thumbnail is (24,12) with opaque content at (7,2) and transparent (0,0). The 400x100 image card is (24,8) and the painted margin at (+1,+1) is checker white (255) | `tst_layers_snapshot::subCanvasLayerPlacement`, `tst_layers_panel_fixes::maskThumbnailKeepsDocumentAspect` |
| `lpr_thumb_mask_aspect` | Create a 400x100 document, paste a 50x50 raster layer at (50,25), add a layer mask, snapshot with Entire Document and with `entireDocument=false`, and paint with Entire Document; also show a 200x20 row with a raster and a vector mask, a 20x200 row, and an in-range 1600x900 vector mask | Entire Document and Layer Bounds: the mask image and card are (24,8) (clamped from 4:1), black at (0,0), white (reveal-all) at (3,2), and the painted mask-card margin at (+1,+1) is black; both placements keep the same clamped mask image and card because both are placement-independent. A mask wider than the canvas is clipped, not scaled. The 200x20 raster and vector cards match the content card; the vector-mask image is (24,8) on 200x20, (8,24) on 20x200, and (24,14) on the in-range 1600x900 document (the shared rounded extent; previously 24x13) | `pictura-app::positioned_mask_thumbnail_follows_document_aspect`, `pictura-app::extreme_document_mask_thumbnail_aspect_is_clamped`, `pictura-app::extreme_document_vector_mask_thumbnail_aspect_is_clamped`, `tst_layers_panel_fixes::maskThumbnailKeepsDocumentAspect`, `tst_canvas_mask_domain::allMaskCardsFollowDocumentAspect`, `tst_canvas_mask_domain::extremeAspectThumbnailIsClamped` |
