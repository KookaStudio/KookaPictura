# Proposal

## Why

With every table added, the panel is taller than a laptop screen. It should be
wider and shorter, and carry the product's own name.

## What Changes

- The tables sit in two columns under the header: Pictura Resource Usage and
  Version on the left; Pictura Memory Budget, Pictura Activity and Platform on the
  right. The columns are balanced so the panel is wider than it is tall.
- The panel title reads `Pictura Metrics` (the Help menu command keeps its name).
