# Test plan: align-distribute-shape-layers

| Scenario | Steps | Expected result | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Align and distribute by content | Run the existing engine align tests: align squares by top/right/centre, align one to a selection, align painted content inside a larger layer, align with a position-locked layer, distribute four squares. | Edges meet the union or selection edge; painted content lines up; the locked layer stays; distribute spaces the middles evenly. | `pictura-render::align_lines_edges_up_on_the_outermost`, `pictura-render::align_centres_on_the_middle_of_them_all`, `pictura-render::align_against_a_selection_moves_even_one_layer`, `pictura-render::align_goes_by_the_content_not_the_layer`, `pictura-render::align_leaves_a_position_locked_layer_where_it_is`, `pictura-render::distribute_spaces_the_middle_evenly_and_keeps_the_ends` |
| Shape layers align and distribute by their outlines | In the engine test, add three rectangle shape layers at different positions, check enablement, align them by top, then distribute by left. In `tst_align_distribute`, add three shape layers, select two then three, check the menu and Move options-bar actions, and trigger Align ▸ Top and Distribute ▸ Left. | Shape layers count as content, each outline top becomes the highest, the middle left becomes the even step, the canvas-sized fill rects stay unchanged, and the history labels match. | `pictura-render::shape_layers_align_and_distribute_by_their_outline`, `tst_align_distribute::shapeLayers` |
| Menu commands enable and act on the selected layers | Run `tst_align_distribute` with one, two, and three pixel layers and trigger Align ▸ Top, Distribute ▸ Left, and Align Layers To Selection ▸ Bottom. | Align needs two layers, Distribute three, and Align Layers To Selection a selection; each records its named state and moves the layers. | `tst_align_distribute::menuCommands`, `tst_align_distribute::alignToSelection` |
