# Test plan

All scenarios are automated; no manual reviewer checks.

| Scenario | Steps | Expected result | Coverage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lpr_thumb_aspect` | a regular layer of a document whose width and height differ is shown | the drawn thumbnail's aspect ratio matches the document, with the unused part of the row's thumbnail box left empty | `tst_layers_snapshot::canvasAspectThumbnailRegression` |
| `lpr_thumb_source_aspect` | a layer spanning the full width of a tall document is shown with Entire Document thumbnail contents | the thumbnail image has the document's aspect ratio and its rightmost column is layer content rather than transparent padding | `tst_layers_snapshot::canvasAspectThumbnailRegression` |
| `lpr_thumb_square_card` | an adjustment, fill, type, or content-less synthetic row is shown in a document whose width and height differ | its thumbnail card is 1:1 (width equals height), while a pixel layer's thumbnail is still letterboxed to the document | `tst_layers_snapshot::canvasAspectThumbnailRegression` |
| `lpr_thumb_sub_canvas_layer` | a raster layer smaller than the canvas is shown on a non-square document with Entire Document thumbnail contents | its thumbnail image carries the document's aspect ratio, the layer content sits at its document-relative position, and the surrounding margin is transparent and painted as the checkerboard | `tst_layers_snapshot::canvasAspectThumbnailRegression` |
| `lpr_thumb_mask_aspect` | a raster mask on a pixel, adjustment, fill, type or group layer is shown on a non-square document with Entire Document thumbnail contents | the mask thumbnail image carries the document's aspect ratio, its coverage sits at its document-relative position with opaque black zero-coverage margins, independently of the left content thumbnail card - **AND WHEN** the mask extends past the document bounds - **THEN** the mask thumbnail is clipped to the document rather than scaled down, without changing the stored mask samples - **AND WHEN** the same row is shown with Layer Bounds thumbnail contents - **THEN** the mask image keeps its document aspect and opaque grayscale canvas coverage while its card retains the existing document-aspect layout | `tst_canvas_mask_domain::allMaskCardsFollowDocumentAspect`, `tst_layers_snapshot::maskThumbnailUsesOpaqueCanvasCoverage` |
| `uip_layers_mask_adjacency` | a layer with a mask and a vector mask is shown | the mask thumbnail is directly right of the image thumbnail and the vector mask directly right of the mask, each with a chain glyph | `tst_canvas_mask_domain::allMaskCardsFollowDocumentAspect` |
| `uip_layers_active_thumb` | a layer thumbnail is clicked to become the edit target | brackets mark that thumbnail and a group row never shows brackets on its icon | `tst_canvas_mask_domain::maskThumbnailUsesOnlyBrackets`, `tst_canvas_mask_domain::layerRowClickDefaultsToContent` |
| `uip_layers_link_glyph` | a masked or vector-masked row is shown, linked or unlinked | its chain glyph is drawn two pixels larger than the slot and rotated a quarter turn, so its ink is taller than it is wide | `tst_layers_panel_fixes::linkGlyphRotatedAndLarger` |
| `lpr_thumb_adjustment_icon` | an Invert, Levels, or other adjustment layer is shown | its thumbnail is a neutral card with that adjustment kind's icon and no checkerboard | `tst_layers_panel_fixes::adjustmentThumbnailIsSquareWithoutOutline` |
| `lpr_thumb_fill_preview` | a Solid Color, Gradient, or Pattern fill layer is shown | its thumbnail shows the fill's solid colour, gradient ramp, or scaled pattern tile instead of a generic checkerboard | `tst_layers_panel_fixes::fillThumbnailIsSquareWithOutline` |
| `lpr_thumb_adjustment_square` | an adjustment layer is shown in a document whose width and height differ | its image thumbnail card is 1:1 (width equals height) rather than letterboxed to the document | `tst_layers_panel_fixes::adjustmentThumbnailIsSquareWithoutOutline` |
| `lpr_thumb_fill_square` | a Solid Color, Gradient, or Pattern fill layer is shown in a document whose width and height differ | its card is 1:1, the preview image the bridge supplies is square, and the preview fills the card without a document-aspect margin or distortion | `tst_layers_panel_fixes::fillThumbnailIsSquareWithOutline` |
| `lpr_thumb_mask_square` | an adjustment or fill layer carries a layer mask or a vector mask | the mask and vector-mask cards follow the document aspect while the left content thumbnail keeps its square card | `tst_layers_panel_fixes::maskThumbnailKeepsDocumentAspectOnSquareRows` |
| `mtp_portrait` | portrait and landscape PSD masks are opened at each thumbnail size | their source thumbnails are opaque and their document-aspect cards contain grayscale coverage through every interior edge, with a black outline and no checkerboard strip, including at fractional display scale | `tst_canvas_mask_domain::importedPortraitMaskCardIsOpaque` |
| `mtp_square` | mask previews are displayed on adjustment or fill rows in a portrait document | the mask cards follow the document aspect and their opaque previews fill those cards, while the adjustment or fill content cards stay square | `tst_canvas_mask_domain::allMaskCardsFollowDocumentAspect` |
| `mcd_row_same` | a raster mask is active or soloed and its layer's row body is clicked | the left content thumbnail is active, raster mask editing and solo are cleared, and history is unchanged | `tst_canvas_mask_domain::layerRowClickDefaultsToContent` |
| `mcd_row_other` | a pixel, adjustment, fill, type or group row previously had a raster or separately displayed vector mask active, another row is selected, and its row body is clicked again | the revisited row's left content thumbnail is active instead of a remembered mask target | `tst_canvas_mask_domain::layerRowClickDefaultsToContent` |
| `mcd_explicit_mask` | a raster or vector mask thumbnail is clicked directly | the clicked mask remains the active thumbnail rather than the default content thumbnail | `tst_canvas_mask_domain::layerRowClickDefaultsToContent` |
| `mcd_creation` | a new adjustment layer with a mask is created while another layer's content thumbnail is active | the new adjustment is selected and its mask thumbnail is active in the normal single creation history state | `tst_canvas_mask_domain::adjustmentCreationActivatesMask` |
