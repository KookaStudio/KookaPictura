# Test Plan: move-mask-selection-content

| Scenario | Steps | Expected | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mask_content_move` | Engine test builds a mask-local coverage plane and moves it by `(dx, dy)`. Qt test uses `paint_fixture::Fixture`: solid layer, hide-all mask, panel refresh, white patch, Ctrl-A, Move-tool drag. | Mask coverage translates; layer pixels, mask attachment, and mask rectangle are unchanged. | `pictura-render::move_mask_selection_translates_and_fills_background`, `pictura-render::move_mask_selection_leaves_pixels_and_mask_metadata_unchanged`, `pictura-render::move_mask_selection_works_on_a_group_mask`, `tst_mask_edit::moveSelectionOnMaskMovesCoverage` |
| `mask_content_move_background` | Engine test moves a white patch with a black background; a second engine test uses a 50% selection edge; Qt test samples the vacated area. | Vacated coverage moves toward background luma; the layer is hidden there; partial edges blend. | `pictura-render::move_mask_selection_translates_and_fills_background`, `pictura-render::move_mask_selection_blends_partial_coverage`, `tst_mask_edit::moveSelectionOnMaskMovesCoverage` |
| `mask_content_move_duplicate` | Engine test moves with `duplicate = true`; Qt test Alt-drags the mask selection. | Source coverage remains, the copy appears at the offset, no layer is inserted, the active layer is unchanged, the preview is inactive, and one state is recorded. | `pictura-render::move_mask_selection_duplicate_keeps_the_source`, `tst_mask_edit::altDragCopiesMaskCoverage` |
| `mask_content_move_ctrl` | Qt test selects the Marquee tool, holds Ctrl inside the selection, and drags. | Mask coverage moves and no new selection starts. | `tst_mask_edit::ctrlDragOnMaskMovesCoverage` |
| `mask_content_move_history` | Qt test records the history count and label around a non-zero drag, then undoes it. | Exactly one `Move Selection` state, the selection sits at the offset, and undo restores the mask and selection. | `tst_mask_edit::moveSelectionOnMaskMovesCoverage` |
| `mask_content_move_noop` | Qt test presses and releases on the mask target without moving. | No history state and the mask is unchanged. | `tst_mask_edit::moveSelectionOnMaskMovesCoverage` |
| Toggling the thumbnails swaps the pairs | Qt test switches to the mask and back, changing each target's colours. | The mask starts white foreground / black background; the image pair returns on the layer click; the mask pair remembers its changes. | `tst_mask_edit::colorsFollowTheEditTarget` |
| `lpr_mask_reset_colors` | Qt test calls `Toolbox::resetForegroundBackground()` with the mask active and after switching back to the layer. | The mask pair becomes white/black; the image pair becomes black/white. | `tst_mask_edit::resetColorsFollowTheEditTarget` |

Additional non-scenario engine coverage required by the design:
`pictura-render::move_mask_selection_clips_at_the_mask_rect` (offset mask rect and destination clipping), `pictura-render::move_mask_selection_materializes_missing_data` (absent/wrong-length stored plane), `pictura-render::move_mask_selection_refuses_a_mismatched_selection_plane`, and `pictura-render::move_mask_selection_ignores_layer_locks`.
