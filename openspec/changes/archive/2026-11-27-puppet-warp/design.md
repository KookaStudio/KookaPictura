## Context

The Edit catalogue supplies the dormant command. The transform session and raster filter dialogs already establish the separation between transient editing and document history.

## Goals and limits

Use a dialog styled like Pictura's other raster tools, with the isolated target on a checkerboard and a fit-to-window viewport. Support bounded RGB/Grayscale 8-bit raster content and ordinary raster user masks. Vector masks, Smart Objects, high bit depth, and Action replay are deferred.

## Decisions

- Use a regular triangulation with each rest pin inserted by subdividing the grid axes. Every pin is a vertex and lands exactly at its target. For each unpinned vertex blend pin-local rigid transforms using normalized inverse-square rest-distance weights; Mode changes the falloff exponent (Rigid 1, Normal 2, Distort 4). A pin-local transform rotates around its rest position then translates it to its target. Manual rotation uses the selected angle. In Auto, calculate the circular mean of the signed changes in direction from that pin to every other pin, weighted by inverse-square rest distance; ignore coincident or collapsed neighbor vectors and use zero when there are no usable neighbors or the mean is indeterminate. Thus a single Auto pin translates rigidly. Manual and Auto rotation affect unpinned vertices, never the exact pin constraints. This is an independently designed approximation, not a claim about Adobe's solver.
- Inverse-map covered destination pixel centers through triangles and sample the source with premultiplied bilinear interpolation. Each pin receives a stable creation ordinal and is inserted at the front of a total depth order; forward/backward swaps it with its adjacent pin, and moving past an end is a no-op. Assign each triangle to its nearest rest pin at its rest centroid, breaking equal distances by earlier creation ordinal. A triangle assigned to a pin farther forward wins an overlap; with no pins or equal owner, the lower stable rest-triangle index wins. Use the same ordering and triangles for preview and commit; shared edges use a consistent half-open coverage rule.
- Fit preview using an isolated target. Raster preview and commit use the same triangle mapping at different sample scales. Cap the full-size editable source and destination at four mebipixels each, the preview at one mebipixel, pins at 32, and generated vertices at 262,144. Before allocating or rasterizing, use checked arithmetic to reject non-finite or malformed geometry, excessive dimensions, vertices, or more than 64 million candidate pixel/triangle bounding-box visits per render; include expansion, inserted grid axes, and clipping in these calculations. Refusal leaves the dialog available for adjustment. Expansion pads the mesh around the target and uses transparent or default-mask samples outside it.
- Linked raster masks and their color layers share one mesh over their bounded union, with transparent color and default-mask padding. Preview applies enabled linked-mask coverage and density to the raster alpha; ponytail: mask feather is omitted from the isolated preview, though the committed layer retains its feather and the document compositor applies it. Targeting the mask itself edits only the mask. Eight-bit retained native stores are admitted for RGB/Grayscale source modes; color replacement retires the store, while mask-only replacement removes its stale mask plane. Unmodeled real-mask channels remain unsupported.
- Keep the original target and revision for the dialog lifetime. The bridge publishes one replacement with the existing history transaction, rechecking document identity, binding, revision, and eligibility before writing. Identity, cancellation, stale targets, or refusal writes nothing. Only final pixels or mask values save into PSD/PSB.
- Alt-click directly on a pin deletes it; Alt-drag on the nearby rotation ring changes its manual angle. Holding H hides pins only and releasing H restores their previous visibility; Show Mesh independently controls mesh display. Shift-click selects multiple pins and Shift-drag moves the selected pins together; Ctrl+A selects all pins, Ctrl+D deselects them, and Ctrl+Z undoes the last pin adjustment within the dialog without changing document history. Adobe documents temporary hiding and the rotation ring in its [Puppet Warp guide](https://helpx.adobe.com/ca/photoshop/desktop/effects-filters/artistic-stylize-filters/distort-specific-image-areas-with-puppet-warp.html) and [keyboard shortcuts](https://helpx.adobe.com/content/dam/help/en/pdf/photoshop_reference.pdf).

## Risks and trade-offs

Large moves can fold triangles. The explicit total depth order and stable tie-break make the result reproducible, but do not claim Photoshop parity. Restrict unsupported modes, layer kinds, selections and lock states rather than silently editing an unintended target. The bounded work budget can refuse a complex mesh even when its raster dimensions are within the area limits.
