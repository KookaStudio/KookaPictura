# Puppet Warp test plan

| Scenario | Steps | Expected result | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| `puppet-native` | Admit a retained 8-bit layer and mask; replace each surface. | Correct samples are retained or invalidated without resurrecting stale planes. | `pictura-app::retained_eight_bit_and_linked_mask_targets` |
| `puppet-linked` | Warp a layer with a linked mask, then undo. | Preview shows linked mask coverage and density; both surfaces deform with one mesh and one undo restores them. | `tst_puppet_warp::linkedMaskApplyUndo`, `pictura-app::linked_mask_union_pads_each_surface`, `pictura-app::linked_preview_applies_mask_density_to_alpha` |
| `puppet-target` | Compare a supported raster and mask with locked, hidden, Background, selected, unsupported and oversized targets. | Edit > Puppet Warp reflects eligibility. | `tst_puppet_warp::targetEligibility` |
| `puppet-anchor` | Solve with one moved vertex and one stationary pin. | Both positions are exact and an unpinned vertex moves. | `pictura-ops::pins_constrain_vertices` |
| `puppet-anchor-area` | Use Normal mode, two manual zero-rotation pins 100 px apart, one stationary and the other moved 10 px; inspect points within 1 px of the anchor. | Every inspected point moves less than 1 px. | `pictura-ops::stationary_anchor_neighborhood` |
| `puppet-controls` | Change controls, Alt-drag a pin ring, hold and release H, Shift-click and Shift-drag multiple pins, use Ctrl+A, Ctrl+D and Ctrl+Z, use H after focusing a control, and Alt-click the pin. | Preview updates, H hides only pins until release, multi-pin selection, movement and local undo work, Alt-click removes the pin, and document history is unchanged. | `tst_puppet_warp::meshControls` |
| `puppet-auto-gesture` | Move two Auto pins and Alt-drag the rotation ring slightly. | The manual angle starts at the nonzero Auto angle. | `tst_puppet_warp::autoRotationGesture` |
| `puppet-rotation` | Solve no-neighbor, two-neighbor, degenerate-neighbor and manual-angle fixtures. | Auto circular mean and fallback, manual angle, and exact constraints match the design. | `pictura-ops::pin_rotation_modes` |
| `puppet-fold` | Render overlapping triangles, reorder owners, and exercise equal-distance and same-owner ties at preview and full scale. | Front ordering and ties are deterministic and agree between preview and commit. | `pictura-ops::fold_depth_order`, `tst_puppet_warp::foldPreviewCommit` |
| `puppet-sampling` | Warp numeric translucent-edge and default-mask fixtures, including out-of-bounds samples. | Samples and transformed bounds match expected premultiplied bilinear and default-mask values; preview and commit correspond at matching sample positions. | `pictura-ops::warp_sampling_and_bounds`, `tst_puppet_warp::samplingPreviewCommit` |
| `puppet-transaction` | Cancel a changed preview, apply another, then undo. | Cancel preserves document data and history, Apply adds one state and Undo restores the source. | `tst_puppet_warp::applyCancelUndo` |
| `puppet-mask` | Activate a raster mask, move its samples, and undo. | Color data stays intact and undo restores mask samples and bounds. | `tst_puppet_warp::maskApplyUndo` |
| `puppet-bound` | Submit oversized source, output, vertex and work-budget fixtures; attempt refused output and identity through the dialog. | Refusal precedes excessive allocation or work; no pixels, bounds or history change. | `pictura-ops::oversized_mesh_is_refused`, `tst_puppet_warp::refusedOrIdentityAddsNoHistory` |
| `puppet-stale` | Change document, target binding, revision, or eligibility while the dialog is open, then Apply. | Each attempt leaves pixels, geometry and history untouched. | `tst_puppet_warp::staleTargetRefused` |
| Desktop smoke | Warp a visible raster with a linked mask. Place two pins, drag one, hold and release H, Alt-drag the rotation ring, then Apply and Undo. | The desktop delivers the modifier gesture, the isolated preview updates, and Undo restores the layer and mask. | manual: offscreen Qt tests cannot check whether the desktop window manager intercepts Alt-drag. |
