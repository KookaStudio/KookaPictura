# codec/camera-matrices Specification

## Purpose
Colour for vendor raw files that embed no matrix: per-camera XYZ → camera matrices fitted from openly licensed spectral sensitivities, looked up by make and model, and the as-shot white balance a CR2 records, so the camera colour stage (#398) maps them (#1465).

## Requirements

### Requirement: Vendor raw files take fitted per-camera matrices by make and model

A vendor raw reader SHALL fill the image's colour tags from a generated table
of per-camera XYZ → camera matrices when the file's make and model name a
camera in it: `ColorMatrix1` and `ColorMatrix2` under
`CalibrationIlluminant1` and `CalibrationIlluminant2` set to the EXIF
`LightSource` code the table gives each matrix. A spectral fit's matrices
SHALL be under 17 (CIE A) and 21 (D65). Model names SHALL match after
upper-casing, treating `-` as a space, and dropping a leading make word and
the words `EOS` and `DIGITAL`, so a camera's market names match one row. A
camera not in the table SHALL keep no matrices, and opening it SHALL stay
refused as unsupported camera colour. The table SHALL be generated by
`scripts/gen-camera-matrices.py` by fitting openly licensed camera spectral
sensitivities against the CIE 1931 observer, or, for the bodies of the
camera-JPEG requirements, from their recorded fits; no Adobe-derived matrix
SHALL be used. The spectral rows and their proxies SHALL be written to
`camera_matrices.rs` and the camera-JPEG rows and their proxies to
`camera_jpeg_matrices.rs`, looked up in that order. Parity with Adobe Camera
Raw is approximate.

#### Scenario: Known Canon models get both matrices [camera_matrices_lookup]

- **WHEN** synthetic CR2s name make "Canon" and the models "Canon EOS 5DS", "Canon EOS 5DS R", "Canon EOS 400D DIGITAL", "Canon EOS DIGITAL REBEL XTi", "Canon EOS Kiss Digital X" and "Canon EOS-1Ds Mark III"
- **THEN** each has both colour matrices with illuminants 17 and 21; the 5DS and 5DS R matrices are equal, the three 400D names give one matrix pair, and it differs from the 5DS's

#### Scenario: An unknown camera keeps no matrices [camera_matrices_unknown]

- **WHEN** synthetic CR2s name "Canon EOS 450D", a "Nikon" make with model "EOS 5DS", or no make and model
- **THEN** none has a colour matrix, and `decode_raw_source` refuses the 450D with a message containing "camera colour" and "Canon EOS 450D"

#### Scenario: Fitted matrices keep white neutral [camera_matrices_white]

- **WHEN** every table camera's as-shot transform is built for an `AsShotNeutral` equal to its D65 matrix's response to the D65 white, and then its A matrix's response to the A white
- **THEN** each neutral is positive and the reported white balance is 6504 K and then 2856 K, each within 2 %

#### Scenario: The table is reproducible [camera_matrices_generated]

- **WHEN** `scripts/gen-camera-matrices.py` is run on rawtoaces-data at the pinned commit and the CIE D65 table
- **THEN** it rewrites `camera_matrices.rs` and `camera_jpeg_matrices.rs` byte for byte

### Requirement: A CR2 reports its as-shot white balance

The CR2 reader SHALL set `AsShotNeutral` from the Canon maker note's colour
data (tag `0x4001`), read as 16-bit words: SHORT values, or BYTE and
UNDEFINED bytes taken in pairs in the file's byte order (an odd byte
count is unusable). The as-shot RGGB
multipliers start at entry 25 for a 582-entry array, 34 for a 653-entry
array, and otherwise by entry 0 as a signed version: 63 for versions 1 to 15,
71 for 16 to 19 and negative versions, 85 for 32 and 33, and 105 for 34, 48
and 64 to 67. The neutral SHALL be `(G/R, 1, G/B)` with `G` the mean of the
two green multipliers. Colour data of any other type, any other layout, an
array too short, or a multiplier that is not positive SHALL leave
`AsShotNeutral` empty without failing the read.

#### Scenario: Colour-data layouts [cr2_as_shot_layouts]

- **WHEN** synthetic CR2s carry colour data of 582 and 653 entries, and of versions 1, 6, 10, 15, 17, -3, -4, 33 and 48, each with multipliers (2000, 1000, 1024, 1500) at its layout's index and other values elsewhere
- **THEN** each reports `AsShotNeutral` (1012/2000, 1, 1012/1500) within 1e-9

#### Scenario: Colour data stored as bytes [cr2_as_shot_bytes]

- **WHEN** a synthetic CR2 carries version -4 colour data with multipliers (2000, 1000, 1024, 1500) at entry 71, written as an UNDEFINED entry of little-endian byte pairs
- **THEN** it reports `AsShotNeutral` (1012/2000, 1, 1012/1500) within 1e-9

#### Scenario: Unusable colour data [cr2_as_shot_refused]

- **WHEN** synthetic CR2s carry colour data of version 25, a version-10 array of 40 entries, version 10 with a zero red multiplier, version 10 written as a LONG entry, and version -4 written as BYTE values of an odd count
- **THEN** each reads with no `AsShotNeutral`

#### Scenario: A known camera's CR2 decodes [cr2_known_camera_decodes]

- **WHEN** a synthetic "Canon EOS 5DS" CR2 with version-12 colour data of multipliers (2208, 1024, 1024, 1967) is decoded with `decode_raw_source`
- **THEN** it decodes, and its as-shot white balance equals `as_shot_transform` of the image's colour tags and lies between 2000 K and 12000 K

#### Scenario: Real camera files take colour [cr2_colour_corpus]

- **WHEN** `PICTURA_RAW_CORPUS` names a directory of user-supplied `.CR2` files
- **THEN** each file from a table camera decodes with `decode_raw_source` and reports an as-shot temperature between 2000 K and 12000 K, and each other file is refused as unsupported camera colour

### Requirement: Nikon NEF bodies take fitted matrices and the as-shot white balance

The generated table SHALL seed the Nikon bodies whose sensitivities
rawtoaces-data publishes (D70, D200, D700, D800E, D810, D850, D3300, D5100,
D5300, D7000 and Z f) with their same-sensor aliases (D800, Z 6_2), under
make key `NIKON`. The NEF reader SHALL set `AsShotNeutral` from the Nikon
maker note's `WB_RBLevels` (0x000C) red and blue multipliers `R` and `B` as
`(1/R, 1, 1/B)`. Without that tag, a colour-balance tag (0x0097) beginning
with ASCII `0100` SHALL give 16-bit R, B, G, G levels at byte 72, in the maker
note's byte order, as `(G/R, 1, G/B)` with `G` their two greens' mean. No
usable tag or a level that is not positive SHALL leave it empty.

#### Scenario: Known Nikon models get both matrices [nef_camera_matrices]

- **WHEN** synthetic NEFs name make "NIKON CORPORATION" and the models "NIKON D850", "NIKON D800", "NIKON D800E", "NIKON Z f" and "NIKON Z 6_2", and one names "NIKON D4"
- **THEN** each but the D4 has both colour matrices with illuminants 17 and 21, the D800 and D800E matrices are equal, the Z f and Z 6_2 matrices are equal, and the D4 has none

#### Scenario: As-shot white balance [nef_as_shot]

- **WHEN** a synthetic "NIKON D850" NEF carries `WB_RBLevels` (1.9, 1.375, 1, 1), another carries a zero red multiplier, another none, and a "NIKON D100" NEF in each byte order carries a version-`0100` colour balance with levels (581, 436, 256, 256)
- **THEN** the first reports `AsShotNeutral` (1/1.9, 1, 1/1.375) within 1e-9 and decodes with `decode_raw_source` at a temperature between 2000 K and 12000 K, the next two have no `AsShotNeutral`, and the D100s report (256/581, 1, 256/436) within 1e-9

#### Scenario: Fitted Nikon matrices keep white neutral [nef_matrices_white]

- **WHEN** every seeded Nikon body's as-shot transform is built for an `AsShotNeutral` equal to its D65 matrix's response to the D65 white, and then its A matrix's response to the A white
- **THEN** the reported white balance is 6504 K and then 2856 K, each within 2 %

#### Scenario: Real Nikon files take colour [nef_colour_corpus]

- **WHEN** `PICTURA_RAW_CORPUS` names a directory of user-supplied `.NEF` files
- **THEN** each read file from a table camera decodes with `decode_raw_source` at an as-shot temperature between 2000 K and 12000 K, and each other read file is refused as unsupported camera colour

### Requirement: Bodies without published sensitivities take a same-sensor sibling's matrices

`scripts/gen-camera-matrices.py` SHALL emit, for each listed proxy body, a
table row under the proxy's own make and model with the fitted matrices of a
seeded body whose sensor is the same or closely related, each row commented
with its basis. The proxies SHALL be the Nikon D100 (as the D70), the Nikon
D7100 (as the D5300, lower confidence) and the Nikon Coolpix P7100 (as the
Canon PowerShot S90). Such matrices are approximate by construction.

#### Scenario: Proxy bodies take their sibling's matrices [camera_matrices_proxies]

- **WHEN** synthetic NEFs name make "NIKON CORPORATION" with "NIKON D100", "NIKON D7100" and "COOLPIX P7100"
- **THEN** their colour matrices equal those of the D70, the D5300 and a synthetic CR2 naming "Canon PowerShot S90" respectively

### Requirement: Panasonic bodies take spectral or camera-JPEG matrices

The table SHALL hold the DC-GX9's spectral fit under 17 and 21. Panasonic
bodies without published sensitivities SHALL take the matrices
`scripts/fit-camera-jpeg-matrices.py` fits from one sample raw file each: the
3 × 3 map from white-balanced camera RGB to linear sRGB, with camera white
mapped to sRGB white, that best reproduces the chromaticities of the JPEG the
camera embedded in the same file (`JpgFromRaw`, tag `0x002E`), over a grid of
blocks without clipped photosites, the worst 15 % trimmed. Each matrix SHALL
be placed under a white-balance preset the file records: the daylight preset
(D55, code 20, else Fine Weather, code 9 or 1) and, when the file has one, the
tungsten preset (code 3, else 24), by a von Kries change of white in camera
space and a Bradford adaptation from D65 to the preset's light. A fit SHALL
be accepted only with at least 60 usable blocks, an a*b* spread of the
scene's colours of at least 6 and a median chromaticity error of at most 4
ΔE; the script SHALL record every sample's file name, SHA-256, error and
verdict in `scripts/camera-jpeg-fits.json`. Same-camera market names SHALL
share their body's row (DMC-TZ100, DMC-TZ101 and DMC-TZ110 the DMC-ZS100's;
DMC-GM1 the DMC-GM1S's). These matrices reproduce the camera maker's
rendering and are approximate.

### Requirement: Canon CR3 bodies take camera-JPEG matrices

CR3 bodies without published sensitivities SHALL take the matrices
`scripts/fit-camera-jpeg-matrices.py` fits, by the method and acceptance
limits of "Panasonic bodies take spectral or camera-JPEG matrices", from one
sample CR3 each, taken from raw.pixls.us or, where those samples are too
grey to fit (the EOS R3, PowerShot V1, EOS M6 Mark II, PowerShot G7 X Mark
III), fail the fit (the EOS R8 Mark II), are missing (the EOS R1) or fit a
pair that does not read back (the R6 Mark III), from owner-supplied files.
The camera rendering SHALL be the largest first sample of a track that is a
JPEG (the full-size JPEG track), make and model SHALL come from `CMT1`, and
the presets from the colour data (tag `0x4001`) of the `CTMD` maker note: by
colour-data version, Daylight at word 136 (versions 16 to 19), 150 (32, 33),
205 (34, 48) or 110 (64 to 67), followed by Shade, Cloudy and Tungsten, each
a record of four RGGB levels ending in the temperature Canon records, five
words long. The daylight matrix SHALL be placed under D55 (code 20) with the
neutral interpolated in mired between the Daylight and Cloudy presets, and
the tungsten matrix under ISO studio tungsten (code 24) when Tungsten is
recorded at 3200 K. When the colour stage's as-shot solve (the pair
interpolated in mired, iterated from 5003 K; the script estimates
temperatures with McCamy's approximation) does not read either matrix's own
light back within 2 %, the script SHALL drop the tungsten matrix, record why,
and keep the daylight matrix alone. Of a body's several accepted samples, the
generator SHALL take the first, in the recorded order, that has both
matrices, and else the first. Regional names SHALL share their body's row
(Kiss M the M50's, Kiss M2 the M50m2's, Rebel T8i and Kiss X10i the 850D's).
No body SHALL take another camera-JPEG body's row as a proxy. These matrices
reproduce Canon's rendering and are approximate.
