# codec/camera-matrices Specification

## Purpose
Colour for vendor raw files that embed no matrix: per-camera XYZ → camera matrices fitted from openly licensed spectral sensitivities, looked up by make and model, and the as-shot white balance a CR2 records, so the camera colour stage (#398) maps them (#1465).

## Requirements

### Requirement: Vendor raw files take fitted per-camera matrices by make and model

A vendor raw reader SHALL fill the image's colour tags from a generated table
of per-camera XYZ → camera matrices when the file's make and model name a
camera in it: `ColorMatrix1` under `CalibrationIlluminant1` 17 (CIE A) and
`ColorMatrix2` under `CalibrationIlluminant2` 21 (D65). Model names SHALL
match after upper-casing, treating `-` as a space, and dropping a leading
make word and the words `EOS` and `DIGITAL`, so a camera's market names match
one row. A camera not in the table SHALL keep no matrices, and opening it SHALL
stay refused as unsupported camera colour. The table SHALL be generated by
`scripts/gen-camera-matrices.py` by fitting openly licensed camera spectral
sensitivities against the CIE 1931 observer; no Adobe-derived matrix SHALL be
used. Parity with Adobe Camera Raw is approximate.

#### Scenario: Known Canon models get both matrices [camera_matrices_lookup]

- **WHEN** synthetic CR2s name make "Canon" and the models "Canon EOS 5DS", "Canon EOS 5DS R", "Canon EOS 400D DIGITAL", "Canon EOS DIGITAL REBEL XTi", "Canon EOS Kiss Digital X" and "Canon EOS-1Ds Mark III"
- **THEN** each has both colour matrices with illuminants 17 and 21; the 5DS and 5DS R matrices are equal, the three 400D names give one matrix pair, and it differs from the 5DS's

#### Scenario: An unknown camera keeps no matrices [camera_matrices_unknown]

- **WHEN** synthetic CR2s name "Canon EOS 450D", a "Nikon" make with model "EOS 5DS", or no make and model
- **THEN** none has a colour matrix, and `decode_raw_source` refuses the 450D with a message containing "camera colour" and "Canon EOS 450D"

#### Scenario: Fitted matrices keep white neutral [camera_matrices_white]

- **WHEN** every table camera's as-shot transform is built for an `AsShotNeutral` equal to its D65 matrix's response to the D65 white, and then its A matrix's response to the A white
- **THEN** each neutral is positive and the reported white balance is 6504 K and then 2856 K, each within 2 %

#### Scenario: The table is reproducible [camera_matrices_generated]

- **WHEN** `scripts/gen-camera-matrices.py` is run on rawtoaces-data at the pinned commit and the CIE D65 table
- **THEN** it rewrites `camera_matrices.rs` byte for byte

### Requirement: A CR2 reports its as-shot white balance

The CR2 reader SHALL set `AsShotNeutral` from the Canon maker note's colour
data (tag `0x4001`): the as-shot RGGB multipliers start at entry 25 for a
582-entry array, 34 for a 653-entry array, and otherwise by entry 0 as a
signed version: 63 for versions 1 to 15, 71 for 16 to 19 and negative
versions, 85 for 32 and 33, and 105 for 34, 48 and 64 to 67. The neutral SHALL
be `(G/R, 1, G/B)` with `G` the mean of the two green multipliers. Any other
layout, an array too short, or a multiplier that is not positive SHALL leave
`AsShotNeutral` empty.

#### Scenario: Colour-data layouts [cr2_as_shot_layouts]

- **WHEN** synthetic CR2s carry colour data of 582 and 653 entries, and of versions 1, 6, 10, 15, 17, -3, 33 and 48, each with multipliers (2000, 1000, 1024, 1500) at its layout's index and other values elsewhere
- **THEN** each reports `AsShotNeutral` (1012/2000, 1, 1012/1500) within 1e-9

#### Scenario: Unusable colour data [cr2_as_shot_refused]

- **WHEN** synthetic CR2s carry colour data of version 25, a version-10 array of 40 entries, and version 10 with a zero red multiplier
- **THEN** each reads with no `AsShotNeutral`

#### Scenario: A known camera's CR2 decodes [cr2_known_camera_decodes]

- **WHEN** a synthetic "Canon EOS 5DS" CR2 with version-12 colour data of multipliers (2208, 1024, 1024, 1967) is decoded with `decode_raw_source`
- **THEN** it decodes, and its as-shot white balance equals `as_shot_transform` of the image's colour tags and lies between 2000 K and 12000 K

#### Scenario: Real camera files take colour [cr2_colour_corpus]

- **WHEN** `PICTURA_RAW_CORPUS` names a directory of user-supplied `.CR2` files
- **THEN** each file from a table camera decodes with `decode_raw_source` and reports an as-shot temperature between 2000 K and 12000 K, and each other file is refused as unsupported camera colour

### Requirement: Nikon NEF bodies take fitted matrices and the as-shot white balance

The generated table SHALL seed the Nikon bodies whose sensitivities
rawtoaces-data publishes (D70, D200, D700, D800E, D810, D850, D3300, D5100,
D5300, D7000 and Z f) with their same-sensor aliases (D800, Z 6_2), under
make key `NIKON`. The NEF reader SHALL set `AsShotNeutral` from the Nikon
maker note's `WB_RBLevels` (0x000C) red and blue multipliers `R` and `B` as
`(1/R, 1, 1/B)`; a missing tag or a multiplier that is not positive SHALL
leave it empty.

#### Scenario: Known Nikon models get both matrices [nef_camera_matrices]

- **WHEN** synthetic NEFs name make "NIKON CORPORATION" and the models "NIKON D850", "NIKON D800", "NIKON D800E", "NIKON Z f" and "NIKON Z 6_2", and one names "NIKON D4"
- **THEN** each but the D4 has both colour matrices with illuminants 17 and 21, the D800 and D800E matrices are equal, the Z f and Z 6_2 matrices are equal, and the D4 has none

#### Scenario: As-shot white balance [nef_as_shot]

- **WHEN** a synthetic "NIKON D850" NEF carries `WB_RBLevels` (1.9, 1.375, 1, 1), another carries a zero red multiplier, and another none
- **THEN** the first reports `AsShotNeutral` (1/1.9, 1, 1/1.375) within 1e-9 and decodes with `decode_raw_source` at a temperature between 2000 K and 12000 K, and the other two have no `AsShotNeutral`

#### Scenario: Fitted Nikon matrices keep white neutral [nef_matrices_white]

- **WHEN** every seeded Nikon body's as-shot transform is built for an `AsShotNeutral` equal to its D65 matrix's response to the D65 white, and then its A matrix's response to the A white
- **THEN** the reported white balance is 6504 K and then 2856 K, each within 2 %

#### Scenario: Real Nikon files take colour [nef_colour_corpus]

- **WHEN** `PICTURA_RAW_CORPUS` names a directory of user-supplied `.NEF` files
- **THEN** each read file from a table camera decodes with `decode_raw_source` at an as-shot temperature between 2000 K and 12000 K, and each other read file is refused as unsupported camera colour
