# Eraser, Dodge, Burn and Sponge

## Eraser

The Eraser rubs out pixels. On the Background layer it paints with the background colour. On other
layers it makes pixels transparent.

![The Eraser options bar](images/eraser-options.png)

Choose a brush size and softness, the same as for the [Brush](tool-brush.md). A soft eraser makes
soft edges. If you may want the pixels back, use a [layer mask](layer-masks.md) instead.

## Dodge, Burn and Sponge

These three tools share one place in the toolbox. They are named after darkroom techniques.

- **Dodge** lightens the area you paint.
- **Burn** darkens it.
- **Sponge** raises or lowers colour saturation.

![The Dodge options bar](images/dodge-options.png)

The options bar has:

- **Range:** which tones to change: Shadows, Midtones or Highlights.
- **Exposure:** how strong each stroke is. Keep it low, around 10% to 20%, and paint
  over an area more than once.
- **Protect Tones** avoids shifting colours when the tool changes brightness.

## Tips

- Dodge and burn on a copy of the layer, or on a layer filled with 50% grey set to
  [Soft Light](blend-modes.md), to stay non-destructive.
- Use a large soft brush for gentle changes.
