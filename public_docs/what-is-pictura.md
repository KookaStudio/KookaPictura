# What is Kooka Pictura?

Kooka Pictura is a free, open-source photo editor for Linux. It works with
layers, so you can paint, retouch, add text and apply effects without changing
the original photo underneath. It is licensed under the GPL v3 or later.

## What you can do with it

- Open and save Photoshop **PSD** and **PSB** files with their layers, masks and
  effects intact.
- Open PNG, JPEG, GIF, BMP, TIFF, WebP, PDF and EPS files, and export to PNG,
  JPEG, TIFF, WebP, BMP, GIF, SVG and PDF.
- Edit with layers, masks, blend modes and layer styles.
- Correct colour and tone with adjustments such as Levels, Curves and Hue/Saturation.
- Apply over a hundred filters, from blurs and sharpening to Liquify and Oil Paint.
- Select, retouch, clone, heal, paint, draw shapes and add text.

## What it is not

Kooka Pictura focuses on photo editing. It does not include 3D, video, cloud
services or support for Adobe plug-ins. Some features are still on the roadmap;
see the [project roadmap](https://github.com/KookaStudio/KookaPictura/blob/master/ROADMAP.md).
